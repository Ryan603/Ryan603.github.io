About me

More than headlines.


I'm Ryan Wangari, a multimedia journalist based in Nairobi. My work sits where journalism, documentary film and audio production meet — the places I believe the most honest human stories live.

From the lakeside communities of Kapiyo to the studios of Ruben FM, and from the Vuka 2025 festival stage to post-production for internationally distributed podcasts.

Every story has someone who hasn't been heard yet. My job is to find them and make the world listen.
